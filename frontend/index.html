Process
How It Works

A simple four-step process that takes your complaint from submission to resolution.

1

Submit Complaint

Fill in the complaint form with your details, category, priority level, and description. Optionally attach a photo for better context.

2

Admin Reviews

The admin receives real-time notifications and reviews all complaints via the dashboard, prioritising high-urgency cases first.

3

Issue Resolution

The admin marks the complaint as resolved once the issue is fixed. You receive an instant status update via the platform.

4

User Feedback

Rate your experience with a 5-star rating and optional comment to help us continuously improve our service quality.
